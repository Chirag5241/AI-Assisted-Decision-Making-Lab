Alternatives to the regret loss

Six ways to score a round, run on the three-action world. Jiang et al. (2014) score an agent on how it ranks the actions and model its noise; four of the six come from there.

The losses

θ = Ux is what the actions are worth, s the human's scores on the shown features, ŷ their pick and y* the best move. τ = 1, the paper's scale.

LossOf one roundWhat it counts
Regretθy* − θŷ The lab's loss. Eq. 6.
Wrong pick1[ ŷ ≠ y* ] The paper's objective. Ignores how much is lost.
Pairs out of order#{ k < l : s and θ order k, l differently } Kendall tau, the log loss of the Mallows model. Counts the whole ranking, not only its top.
Ranked regretΣi ( maxj ≥ i θσ(j) − θσ(i) ) Regret at every place of the human's ranking σ; its first term is regret. The Plackett-Luce log loss of the ranking, at low noise.
Noisy regretΣk pk (θy* − θk) Regret when the pick is a Plackett-Luce draw, p = softmax(s / τ). Smooth, and the margin counts.
Log loss−log py* Cross-entropy of that draw. Smooth. Ignores how much is lost.
