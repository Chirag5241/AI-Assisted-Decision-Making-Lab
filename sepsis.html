Sepsis, from data

The two-action problem on 40,336 ICU patients (PhysioNet/CinC 2019). The labs are the hidden x, the vitals and demographics are the symptoms s, and U is a logistic fit on the labs. The human starts with random beliefs.

  • One decision per patient. Septic (7.3%): 6 h before onset. Others: an hour drawn from the same distribution of hours. Values are carried forward to that hour.

The data and Σ

U from logistic regression

  • 0.678 the linear U used here, labs only. 0.743 gradient boosting on the same labs: the gap is the cost of linearity. 0.745 with the gaps left as gaps: what the ordering pattern says. 0.726 labs and symptoms together: what U leaves out.
  • Across hospitals: fit on A, scored on B, 0.718; B on A, 0.673.
