We analyze the expected graduation regret incurred by $\mathcal{H}$ after graduation ($t > T$) for the general multi-action setting ($K \ge 2$), latent covariance $\Sigma \succ 0$, and residual covariance $\Gamma \succeq 0$.

##### **Graduation Decision and Regret:**
For latent state $x \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma)$, observation $s = Bx$, and belief matrix $H$, the human selects feature subset $F^*(s) := F_{\mathcal{H}}^*(s; H)$ with $|F^*(s)| \le c$ by [[<G> - 2. Multi-Action Test Selection]].
Upon observing true features $x_{F^*(s)}$, the human takes action:
$$ \hat{y}(x) = \arg\max_{k \in [K]} \hat{u}_k\big(F^*(s), x_{F^*(s)}, s; H\big) $$
The ground truth optimal action is $y^*(x) = \arg\max_{k \in [K]} u_k^\top x$. The graduation regret incurred is:
$$ \ell_{\text{grad}}(x, \hat{y}) = \max_{k \in [K]} u_k^\top x - u_{\hat{y}}^\top x = (u_{y^*(x)} - u_{\hat{y}})^\top x $$
The expected graduation regret is $\mathcal{L}_{\text{grad}}(H) := \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma)}\big[ \ell_{\text{grad}}(x, \hat{y}) \big]$.

##### **Pairwise Representation of Multi-Action Regret:**
For any action pair $k, \ell \in [K]$ with $k \neq \ell$:
* The ground truth advantage of action $k$ over action $\ell$ is $D_{k\ell} := d_{k\ell}^\top x$, where $d_{k\ell} = u_k - u_\ell$.
* The human's perceived advantage under feature subset $F$ is $V_{k\ell}(F) := \hat{u}_k(F) - \hat{u}_\ell(F) = w_{k\ell, F}^\top x$, where from [[3. Graduation Regret.md]]:
  $$ w_{k\ell, F} := \Pi^\top p_{k\ell} + (I_n - \Pi^\top) E_F^\top (p_{k\ell})_F, \qquad p_{k\ell} = h_k - h_\ell $$
  with $\Pi = \Sigma B^\top (B \Sigma B^\top)^{-1} B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$.

**Theorem 1 (Pointwise Pairwise Upper Bound):**
For every realized state $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and human prediction $\hat{y}$:
$$ \ell_{\text{grad}}(x, \hat{y}) \;\le\; \sum_{1 \le k < \ell \le K} |d_{k\ell}^\top x| \cdot \mathbb{1}\big[ (d_{k\ell}^\top x) \cdot V_{k\ell}(F^*(s)) < 0 \big] $$

**Proof:**
1. If $\hat{y} = y^*(x)$, the human makes the optimal decision: $\ell_{\text{grad}}(x, \hat{y}) = 0$. Since every term on the RHS is non-negative ($|d_{k\ell}^\top x| \ge 0$ and $\mathbb{1}[\cdot] \ge 0$), $0 \le \text{RHS}$.
2. If $\hat{y} \neq y^*(x)$, let $y^*(x) = a$ and $\hat{y} = b$ with $a \neq b$. The actual loss is $\ell_{\text{grad}} = (u_a - u_b)^\top x = |d_{ab}^\top x| > 0$.
   In the sum over all undirected pairs $k < \ell$, the pair $\{a, b\}$ appears exactly once:
   * Because $y^* = a$, $u_a^\top x > u_b^\top x \implies d_{ab}^\top x > 0$.
   * Because $\hat{y} = b$, $\hat{u}_a < \hat{u}_b \implies V_{ab}(F^*(s)) < 0$.
   * Therefore, $(d_{ab}^\top x) \cdot V_{ab}(F^*(s)) < 0$, and the indicator is 1:
     $$ |d_{ab}^\top x| \cdot \mathbb{1}\big[ (d_{ab}^\top x) \cdot V_{ab}(F^*(s)) < 0 \big] = |d_{ab}^\top x| = \ell_{\text{grad}}(x, \hat{y}) $$
   All other pairs $(k, \ell) \neq (a, b)$ contribute non-negative terms. Thus $\text{LHS} \le \text{RHS}$. $\blacksquare$

**Fact 1 (Bivariate Normal Loss; the Fact of [[3. Graduation Regret]]):**
For any fixed feature set $F$, $D_{k\ell} = d_{k\ell}^\top x$ and $V_{k\ell}(F) = w_{k\ell, F}^\top x$ are zero-mean jointly Gaussian under $x \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma)$.
By Sheppard's Lemma (from [[3. Graduation Regret.md]]):
$$ \mathbb{E}_x\Big[ |d_{k\ell}^\top x| \cdot \mathbb{1}\big[ (d_{k\ell}^\top x) \cdot V_{k\ell}(F) < 0 \big] \Big] = \sqrt{\frac{d_{k\ell}^\top \Sigma d_{k\ell}}{2\pi}} \Big(1 - \rho_{k\ell}(F, p_{k\ell})\Big) $$
where $\rho_{k\ell}(F, p_{k\ell})$ is the Pearson correlation coefficient between $D_{k\ell}$ and $V_{k\ell}(F)$:
$$ \rho_{k\ell}(F, p_{k\ell}) = \frac{d_{k\ell}^\top (\Sigma - \Gamma) p_{k\ell} + (p_{k\ell})_F^\top \Gamma_{F, :} d_{k\ell}}{\sqrt{d_{k\ell}^\top \Sigma d_{k\ell}} \;\; \sqrt{p_{k\ell}^\top (\Sigma - \Gamma) p_{k\ell} + (p_{k\ell})_F^\top \Gamma_{FF} (p_{k\ell})_F}} $$

##### **Observation Space Partition and Active Contenders:**
The observation space $\mathbb{R}^d$ partitions into convex polyhedral cones where specific pairs of actions are the leading contenders:
$$ \mathcal{S}_{k\ell} := \left\{ s \in \mathbb{R}^d : \min\big(h_k^\top \mu(s), h_\ell^\top \mu(s)\big) \ge \max_{r \notin \{k, \ell\}} h_r^\top \mu(s) \right\} $$
Within region $\mathcal{S}_{k\ell}$:
1. Actions $k$ and $\ell$ are the top two choices under $\mathcal{H}$'s prior.
2. By the active-contender pinching of [[<G> - 2. Multi-Action Test Selection]], the feature selection rule is **approximately** constant on the part of the cell where the other actions trail by $\Delta_r(s) \ge 3\sigma_r(F)$:
   $$ F^*(s) \approx F_{k\ell}^* := \arg\max_{|F| \le c} (p_{k\ell})_F^\top \Gamma_{FF} (p_{k\ell})_F $$
   Near the cell boundaries a third action is close and $F^*(s)$ can differ (the exact rule maximizes the full VoI of Claim 1 there).
3. Let $w_{k\ell} := \mathbb{P}(s \in \mathcal{S}_{k\ell})$ denote the probability that an observation lands in cell $\mathcal{S}_{k\ell}$. Note $\sum_{1 \le k < \ell \le K} w_{k\ell} = 1$.

**Theorem 2 (Active Contender Regret Decomposition) — corrected.**
Let $\epsilon := \mathbb{P}\big( \{y^*(x), \hat{y}\} \not\subset \{k, \ell\},\ s \in \mathcal{S}_{k\ell} \text{ for some } k<\ell \big)$ and assume $F^*(s) = F^*_{k\ell}$ on $\mathcal{S}_{k\ell}$ (the pinching regime). Then
$$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{grad}}(H) = \sum_{1 \le k < \ell \le K} \mathbb{E}\Big[ |d_{k\ell}^\top x| \cdot \mathbb{1}\big[(d_{k\ell}^\top x)\, V_{k\ell}(F^*_{k\ell}) < 0\big] \cdot \mathbb{1}\big[s \in \mathcal{S}_{k\ell}\big] \Big] + \mathcal{O}\big(\epsilon \cdot \text{(regret scale)}\big). $$
Each term is a Gaussian integral **truncated to the cell**. It is *not* $w_{k\ell}$ times the Sheppard value $\sqrt{d_{k\ell}^\top \Sigma d_{k\ell}/2\pi}\,(1-\rho_{k\ell})$: that would require the pairwise error event to be independent of the cell, and it is not (both depend on $\mu(s)$). The product form is at best a heuristic.

**Upper bound.** Taking expectations in Theorem 1,
$$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{grad}}(H) \;\le\; \sum_{1 \le k < \ell \le K} \mathbb{E}\Big[|d_{k\ell}^\top x| \cdot \mathbb{1}\big[(d_{k\ell}^\top x)\, V_{k\ell}(F^*(s)) < 0\big]\Big]. $$
Fact 1 evaluates a term in closed form only when the panel is fixed. So the closed-form bound $\sum_{k<\ell} \sqrt{d_{k\ell}^\top \Sigma d_{k\ell}/2\pi}\,\big(1 - \rho_{k\ell}(F, p_{k\ell})\big)$ holds when $F^*(s) \equiv F$ is constant (e.g. $B = 0$, or $K = 2$), not with a different $F^*_{k\ell}$ per pair.

**Proof.** Total expectation over the (a.e.) partition $\{\mathcal{S}_{k\ell}\}$; on $\mathcal{S}_{k\ell}$, outside an event of probability $\epsilon$, both $y^*$ and $\hat{y}$ lie in $\{k,\ell\}$, so $\ell_{\text{grad}} = |d_{k\ell}^\top x|\,\mathbb{1}[(d_{k\ell}^\top x) V_{k\ell} < 0]$. The bound is Theorem 1 in expectation. $\blacksquare$
