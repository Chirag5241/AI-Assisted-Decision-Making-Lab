# <G> - 3. Adaptive Estimation & In-Context Scaffolding (Unknown Parameters)

In operational practice, an educational platform or clinical preceptor does not have telepathic access to the student's initial mental model $H^{(0)}$ or intrinsic plasticity $\eta$.

This note formalizes how $\mathcal{A}$ **learns the student's cognitive state online** in the pure decision-theoretic setting via a two-phase protocol:
1. **Diagnostic Placement Phase ($t \le T_{\text{diag}}$):** Identifying initial belief geometry $H^{(0)}$ via unassisted probing ($F = \emptyset, \alpha = 0$).
2. **Online Sequential Bayesian Tracking ($t > T_{\text{diag}}$):** Tracking belief contraction $\hat{H}^{(t)}$ and plasticity $\hat{\eta}$.
3. **Adaptive Graduation Stopping Time ($\tau$):** Certifying graduation dynamically based on estimated proficiency.

*(Note: Estimating latent user preferences $z \in \mathbb{R}^p$ from conversational telemetry is treated in [[<G> - 6. Latent Preference Alignment & Online Learning]].)*

---

### 1. Phase 1: Unassisted Diagnostic Placement ($t \le T_{\text{diag}}$)

* **Protocol:** During the placement phase, the student is presented with cases with $a_t = 0$ and $\alpha_t = 0$ (no hints, no recommendations).
* **Observation:** The student sees only $s^{(t)} = Bx^{(t)}$ and selects action:
  $$ \hat{y}_t = \arg\max_{k \in [K]} h_k^{(0)\top} \mu(s^{(t)}) $$

#### Polyhedral Slicing Identification
By the Scale and Shift Invariance Theorem ([[<G> - 6. Adaptive Tutoring & Placement Test.md]]), the student's choices slice the belief space with $K-1$ linear inequalities per diagnostic round:
$$ (h_{\hat{y}_t}^{(0)} - h_\ell^{(0)})^\top \mu(s^{(t)}) \ge 0 \quad \forall \ell \in [K] \setminus \{\hat{y}_t\} \iff p_{\hat{y}_t \ell}^{(0)\top} \mu(s^{(t)}) \ge 0 $$

The space of candidate belief matrices consistent with $T_{\text{diag}}$ diagnostic observations is the convex polyhedral cone:
$$ \mathcal{H}_{\text{consistent}} = \left\{ H \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times n} : (h_{\hat{y}_t} - h_\ell)^\top \mu(s^{(t)}) \ge 0 \quad \forall t \in [T_{\text{diag}}], \; \ell \in [K] \right\} $$

#### Regularized Margin Estimator
To account for bounded human response noise (trembling temperature $\sigma_{\text{noise}} > 0$), $\mathcal{A}$ recovers $\hat{H}^{(0)}$ by solving a regularized multiclass margin minimization over $T_{\text{diag}} = \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{K \cdot n}{\epsilon^2}\right)$ items:
$$ \hat{H}^{(0)} = \arg\min_H \sum_{t=1}^{T_{\text{diag}}} \max_{\ell \ne \hat{y}_t} \Big( 0, \; 1 + (h_\ell - h_{\hat{y}_t})^\top \mu(s^{(t)}) \Big) + \frac{\gamma_{\text{reg}}}{2} \|H\|_F^2 $$
This recovers the normalized pairwise difference directions $\bar{p}_{k\ell}^{(0)}$ along all action margins.

### 2. Phase 2: Online Sequential Tracking of Beliefs and Plasticity ($t > T_{\text{diag}}$)

Once tutoring begins, $\mathcal{A}$ does not know the exact intrinsic plasticity rate $\eta \in (0, 1)$.

At each lesson round $t$:
1. The controller emits control tuple $M_t = (\alpha_t, a_t, \hat{k}_t)$.
2. The user observes $s^{(t)}$ and advice $M_t$, selecting action $\hat{y}_t \in [K]$.

#### The Observation Likelihood
The probability of the student choosing action $k$ follows a Multinomial Logit choice model with rational trembling temperature $\sigma > 0$:
$$ P\big(\hat{y}_t = k \;\big|\; s^{(t)}, M_t, H^{(t)}\big) = \frac{\exp\left( \frac{1}{\sigma} \hat{u}_k(s^{(t)}, M_t; H^{(t)}) \right)}{\sum_{\ell=1}^K \exp\left( \frac{1}{\sigma} \hat{u}_\ell(s^{(t)}, M_t; H^{(t)}) \right)} $$

#### The State Transition Model
Under active engagement $g_{t, j} = (1 - \alpha_t) a_{t, j}$, the true belief matrix evolves as:
$$ H_{kj}^{(t+1)} - U_{kj} = (1 - \eta \cdot g_{t, j}) \big( H_{kj}^{(t)} - U_{kj} \big) + w_{kj}^{(t)}, \qquad w^{(t)} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_w^2 I) $$
where $w^{(t)}$ represents stochastic drift or mental fatigue.

#### Sequential Particle / Kalman Filter Update
Let $\Theta_t = (H^{(t)}, \eta)$ be the joint latent state. $\mathcal{A}$ maintains a recursive posterior belief density $q_t(\Theta) = P(\Theta \mid \hat{y}_{1:t}, M_{1:t})$:
$$ q_t(\Theta) \propto P\big(\hat{y}_t \;\big|\; s^{(t)}, M_t, H^{(t)}\big) \int P\big(\Theta \;\big|\; \Theta_{t-1}, M_{t-1}\big) q_{t-1}(\Theta_{t-1}) \, d\Theta_{t-1} $$

**Online Control via Certainty-Equivalence:**
At each round $t$, $\mathcal{A}$:
1. Computes posterior expectations $\hat{H}^{(t)} = \mathbb{E}_{q_t}[H^{(t)}]$ and $\hat{\eta}_t = \mathbb{E}_{q_t}[\eta]$.
2. Evaluates the estimated Preceptor Index:
   $$ \hat{\nu}_j(t) = \frac{\delta^{T-t}}{1-\delta} \hat{\tilde{\eta}}_t e^{-\hat{\tilde{\eta}}_t G_j(t-1)} \mathcal{S}_j\big(\hat{H}^{(t)}\big) $$
3. Executes the water-filling attention $a_t^*$ and Socratic switch $\alpha_t^*$ derived in [[<G> - 2. Optimal Scaffolding Policy (Known Parameters)]].

### 3. Adaptive Graduation Stopping Time ($\tau$)

Instead of an arbitrary fixed horizon $T$, $\mathcal{A}$ endogenizes the graduation boundary as a stopping time $\tau$.

#### Proficiency Certification Criterion
Let $\epsilon_{\text{cert}} > 0$ be the maximum acceptable unassisted graduation regret.
$\mathcal{A}$ tracks the posterior expected graduation regret:
$$ \widehat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{grad}}(t) := \mathbb{E}_{q_t}\left[ \mathcal{L}_{\text{grad}}\big(H^{(t)}\big) \right] $$

#### The Pure Economic Stopping Rule
Every additional training round incurs expected student error cost $\lambda \mathbb{E}[\ell_{\text{train}}]$.
Define the **Estimated Marginal Pedagogical Gain**:
$$ \widehat{\Delta}_j(t) := \widehat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{grad}}(t) - \mathbb{E}\left[ \widehat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{grad}}(t+1) \;\Big|\; a_t = e_j, \alpha_t = 0 \right] $$

**Stopping Condition:**
The algorithm triggers **Graduation Certification ($\tau = t$)** at the earliest round where:
$$ \max_{j \in [n]} \widehat{\Delta}_j(t) \;\le\; \frac{1-\delta}{\delta} \lambda \mathbb{E}[\ell_{\text{train}}] \quad \text{OR} \quad \widehat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{grad}}(t) \le \epsilon_{\text{cert}} $$

When the stopping condition is satisfied:
1. The assistant disengages all scaffolding ($M_t = \emptyset$).
2. For all $t > \tau$, $\mathcal{H}$ acts unassisted, achieving provably minimal lifetime regret.
