# <G> - 1. Problem Formulation & Two-Stage Architecture

This note formalizes the **pure decision-theoretic problem** of Pedagogical AI Assistance with an LLM, establishing the mathematical foundations for natural language advice, cognitive offloading, and the Two-Stage (Controller + Verbalizer) architecture.

*(Note: Human preferences and latent style alignment are treated as an orthogonal extension in [[<G> - 6. Latent Preference Alignment & Online Learning]].)*

---

### 1. Environment & Underlying Decision Task

We consider an agent/human $\mathcal{H}$ interacting with a sequential decision environment over a training phase $t \in \{1, \dots, T\}$ followed by an unassisted **graduation phase** $t > T$:
* **Latent State:** Realized instance $x^{(t)} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma) \in \mathbb{R}^n$.
* **Coarse Observation:** $\mathcal{H}$ and the assistant observe $s^{(t)} = Bx^{(t)} \in \mathbb{R}^d$, where $B \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times n}$.
* **Conditional Expectation:** $\mu(s) = \Pi x$ where $\Pi = \Sigma B^\top (B \Sigma B^\top)^{-1} B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, and residual covariance $\Gamma = \Sigma - \Pi \Sigma \succeq 0$.
* **Ground Truth Utilities:** $U \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times n}$ with rows $u_1, \dots, u_K \in \mathbb{R}^n$. The true optimal action is $y^*(x) = \arg\max_{k \in [K]} u_k^\top x$.
* **Human Belief Matrix:** At round $t$, $\mathcal{H}$ maintains an internal mental model $H^{(t)} \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times n}$ with rows $h_1^{(t)}, \dots, h_K^{(t)} \in \mathbb{R}^n$.

---

### 2. The Semantic Advice Space $M_t$

Rather than modeling unconstrained text tokens in the core optimization, we model the **semantic information payload** of the advice message $M_t$:
$$ M_t = \big(\alpha_t, \; a_t, \; \hat{k}_t\big) $$
where:
* **$\alpha_t \in [0, 1]$ (Scaffolding Degree):** Continuous spectrum between directive and Socratic guidance.
  * $\alpha_t = 1$: **Pure Directive** (The assistant explicitly tells the user what to do).
  * $\alpha_t = 0$: **Pure Socratic** (The assistant withholds the answer and provides only attention cues).
* **$a_t \in [0, 1]^n$ with $\|a_t\|_1 \le C$ (Attentional Saliency Vector):**
  * $a_{t, j} \in [0, 1]$ is the degree of cognitive focus directed toward feature $j$.
  * Capacity constraint: $\sum_{j=1}^n a_{t, j} \le C$, where $C \in (0, 1]$ represents working-memory bandwidth.
* **$\hat{k}_t \in [K]$ (Recommended Action):** The ground truth optimal action $y^*(x)$ or Bayes-optimal action under symptoms $k^*(s) = \arg\max_k u_k^\top \mu(s)$.

---

### 3. Human Decision Rule under Advice

Upon observing $s^{(t)}$ and receiving prompt $M_t = (\alpha_t, a_t, \hat{k}_t)$, the human forms a blended perceived utility for each action $k \in [K]$:
$$ \hat{u}_k\big(s, M_t; H^{(t)}\big) = (1 - \alpha_t) \underbrace{\left[ \sum_{j=1}^n a_{t, j} H_{kj}^{(t)} x_j + \sum_{j=1}^n (1 - a_{t, j}) H_{kj}^{(t)} \mu_j(s) \right]}_{\text{Autonomous / Socratic Evaluation } \hat{u}_{k, \text{soc}}} \;+\; \alpha_t \underbrace{\Omega \cdot \mathbb{1}[k = \hat{k}_t]}_{\text{Directive Recommendation Pull}} $$
where $\Omega > 0$ is the perceived authority of the advice.
The human executes:
$$ \hat{y}_t = \arg\max_{k \in [K]} \hat{u}_k\big(s^{(t)}, M_t; H^{(t)}\big) $$

#### The Binary Reduction ($K = 2$)
Let $d = u_1 - u_2$, $p^{(t)} = h_1^{(t)} - h_2^{(t)}$, and let recommended action $\hat{k}_t \in \{1, 2\}$ align with $\operatorname{sign}(d^\top x)$. The net perceived value difference between Action 1 and Action 2 is:
$$ V_t(\alpha_t, a_t) = (1 - \alpha_t) \left[ p^{(t)\top} \mu(s) + \sum_{j=1}^n a_{t, j} p_j^{(t)} \big(x_j - \mu_j(s)\big) \right] + \alpha_t \Omega \cdot \operatorname{sign}(\hat{k}_t) $$
The human chooses Action 1 iff $V_t \ge 0$.
* If $\alpha_t \to 1$: $V_t \approx \Omega \operatorname{sign}(\hat{k}_t)$, guaranteeing correct action ($\ell_{\text{train}} = 0$).
* If $\alpha_t \to 0$: The human decides based entirely on their own weights $p^{(t)}$ evaluated on features highlighted by $a_t$.

---

### 4. The Cognitive Offloading Learning Dynamic

Learning requires active cognitive processing. If the human merely follows a directive recommendation ($\alpha_t \approx 1$), they experience **cognitive offloading**: their internal weights do not update.

Only the autonomous/Socratic component $(1 - \alpha_t) a_{t, j}$ drives belief contraction:
$$ H_{kj}^{(t+1)} - U_{kj} = \Big( 1 - (1 - \alpha_t) a_{t, j} \eta \Big) \big( H_{kj}^{(t)} - U_{kj} \big) \quad \forall k \in [K], \; j \in [n] $$
Along any action margin $(k, \ell)$, the pairwise difference vector contracts as:
$$ p_{k\ell, j}^{(t+1)} - d_{k\ell, j} = \Big( 1 - (1 - \alpha_t) a_{t, j} \eta \Big) \big( p_{k\ell, j}^{(t)} - d_{k\ell, j} \big) $$

#### Continuous Active Engagement
Define the **instantaneous active engagement** on feature $j$ as:
$$ g_{t, j} := (1 - \alpha_t) a_{t, j} \in [0, 1] $$
Cumulative active engagement on feature $j$ after $t$ rounds is:
$$ G_j(t) = \sum_{\tau=1}^t (1 - \alpha_\tau) a_{\tau, j} $$
Under continuous engagement, the belief contraction after $t$ rounds is:
$$ p_{k\ell, j}^{(t)} - d_{k\ell, j} = (1 - \eta)^{G_j(t)} \big( p_{k\ell, j}^{(0)} - d_{k\ell, j} \big) =: \psi(G_j(t)) \big( p_{k\ell, j}^{(0)} - d_{k\ell, j} \big) $$
where $\psi(G) = (1 - \eta)^G = e^{-\tilde{\eta} G}$ with $\tilde{\eta} = -\ln(1 - \eta) > 0$.

> **Key Theoretical Bridge:** The discrete exposure count $m_j(t) = \sum \mathbb{1}[j \in F_\tau]$ in classic algorithmic assistance is generalized to the **continuous active cognitive engagement** $G_j(t) = \sum (1 - \alpha_\tau) a_{\tau, j}$.

---

### 5. The Pure Dynamic Optimization Objective

The algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ selects the sequence of advice tuples $\pi = (M_1, \dots, M_T)$ to minimize:
$$ \mathcal{L}(\pi) := \sum_{t=1}^T \lambda \mathbb{E}\big[\ell_{\text{train}}(x^{(t)}, \hat{y}_t(M_t))\big] \;+\; \frac{\delta}{1-\delta} \mathcal{L}_{\text{grad}}\big(H^{(T)}\big) $$
where $\lambda \in [0, 1]$ is the cost of mistakes during training, $\delta \in (0, 1)$ is the post-graduation discount factor, and unassisted graduation regret $\mathcal{L}_{\text{grad}}$ is determined by Sheppard's formula.

---

### 6. The Two-Stage Architecture (Controller + Verbalizer)

To guarantee mathematical optimality while allowing rich, steerable natural language generation, the system decouples into two modular stages:

```
[ Case s^(t), History H^(t) ]
               │
               ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STAGE 1: THE MATHEMATICAL CONTROLLER                   │
│  Solves the decision-theoretic POMDP / Bellman index:   │
│  Computes optimal control vector:                      │
│      M_t* = (α_t*, a_t*, k_t*)                         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
               │
               ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STAGE 2: ALIGNMENT & VERBALIZER (LLM)                 │
│  Given M_t* and learned latent user preference ẑ_t:    │
│  Generates user-facing natural language text y_t       │
│  enforcing (α_t*, a_t*) while maximizing alignment.    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
               │
               ▼
      User Reads Text Advice
```
